Bill Reminder Privacy Policy

Colbryan Technologies publishes Bill Reminder. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Bills and reminders

The app saves the bill names, prices, renewal dates, and billing cycles you enter in a local database on your device. It uses these details to show your upcoming bills and schedule notifications. Colbryan Technologies does not receive the bill entries you save in the app.

You can remove individual bills within the app and turn off notifications in Android settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes the local bills. The app does not back up your bill database to a Colbryan service.

Purchases

Google Play processes the optional lifetime unlock. The app uses Google Play Billing to confirm and restore access. It saves the unlock status on your device. Google handles payment details, purchase records, and data used to operate Google Play under its own privacy policy. Colbryan Technologies does not receive your card details through Bill Reminder.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, email ejike@colbryan.com. We use information you send us to respond to your request.
